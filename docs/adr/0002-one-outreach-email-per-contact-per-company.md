# One Outreach Email per Contact per company, not per role

When the user has several Applications at one company, they form a Company Group. Each Contact gets at most one Outreach Email that names every role in the group, and the group takes one Shortlist slot. A Contact already emailed within the last 30 days is flagged for review instead of drafted again, even for a new role. Recruiters usually see all of a candidate's applications in the same ATS profile, and separate near-identical emails per role read as spam. Recruiter guidance found on 2026-11-12 favours one message naming the roles over one per role.

## Considered Options

- **Dedupe per Contact per role (the original brief).** Rejected: two roles at Affirm would send the same recruiter two emails on the same day.
