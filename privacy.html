Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026. This policy covers Jobright Outreach (the "app"), the open-source tool at github.com/RottenBanana420/jobright-outreach-agent.

Who has your data

The app runs only on your own computer. It has no server, and its author receives none of your data. Everything it stores stays in files on your computer: a local database of the emails it wrote and sent, the files for each run, and your Gmail sign-in token.

Google user data the app accesses

With your permission (the gmail.modify scope), the app:

  • creates and updates drafts of the outreach emails you review;
  • sends a draft only after you approve it on the local review page;
  • searches your Sent folder for one specific message ID, to confirm whether an email went out before it retries;
  • creates an "Outreach" label and applies it to the emails it sent;
  • reads your Gmail address, to confirm which account you signed in with.

It does not read the content of your inbox or other emails.

How the data is used and shared

Gmail data is used only to provide the features above, for you. It isn't sold, used for advertising, used to train AI models, or shared with anyone. The emails the app writes are composed with Claude Code, which you run under your own Anthropic account. That use is covered by Anthropic's terms, not by this app.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on your computer until you delete it. To remove the app's access to Gmail, delete its sign-in token and remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Emails already in your Gmail stay there, and you manage them in Gmail.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/RottenBanana420/jobright-outreach-agent/issues.
